## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Two audiences buy the same units and need different reassurance:

1. **Addis Ababa professionals and families** evaluating a premium residence in person. They can visit a site, ask neighbours, and judge a finished building with their own eyes.
2. **The Ethiopian diaspora in the US, UK and Europe**, buying remotely, often without visiting before handover. They cannot inspect anything, are wary of Ethiopian developers who take deposits and never deliver, and communicate primarily over WhatsApp across timezones.

A third, smaller audience: commercial tenants and leasing enquiries for the Phase 3 recreation and commercial centre.

## Product Purpose

Hill Bottom Properties develops and sells residential and mixed-use property in Addis Ababa. The website's job is to make a high-value, high-risk purchase feel verifiable: present the portfolio, prove delivery, and route the visitor into a real sales conversation (WhatsApp, call, site visit, or form).

## Positioning

Hill Bottom has something most Ethiopian developers cannot show: **a completed, occupied project.** Hill Bottom Village Block A in Ayat is finished and lived in, and prospective buyers are actively invited to visit it and speak to residents before committing to anything else. The company's own words: "We don't sell renders. We sell execution." Payment milestones are tied to verified construction progress rather than calendar dates.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof outranks aspiration.** A finished, occupied building is the strongest thing this company owns; the site leads with delivery status, not lifestyle mood.
2. **State the status of everything.** Every project carries Complete / In Progress / Opening-date as a first-class fact, never a decorative badge.
3. **Design for someone who cannot visit.** Every claim a remote buyer would want to verify has a path to verification: a tour, a document, a person on WhatsApp.
4. **Measured, not marketed.** The company's own material is drawings, areas, and milestones. The site speaks that language rather than dressing it up.
5. **One conversation away, always.** WhatsApp and a call are reachable from any scroll position, on any page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Bilingual reality: content is English with Amharic appearing in real testimonials. Amharic must render correctly (Ethiopic script support in the font stack).
- Diaspora users are frequently on mobile over variable connections — image weight and no-JS resilience matter.
- WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion honoured.
